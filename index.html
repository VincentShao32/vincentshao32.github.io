Vincent Shao

ML research at Ai2 · University of Washington

I'm Vincent, an undergrad studying computer science at the University of Washington. I'm interested in ML research in computer vision, robotics, and NLP.

I'm currently a student researcher at Ai2 in the robotics team. We recently released RoboTrack, a benchmark and training suite for point tracking in real-world robotic manipulation. I've also contributed to the Molmo line of models, improving object tracking and multi-view pointing.

Next summer I'll be an MLE intern with Jane Street, and I've previously interned at Databricks and AWS. Some of my hobbies are climbing, skiing, and making food!
